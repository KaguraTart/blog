## Let’s tell a story first

Xiao Lin has an interview next Wednesday. He had been preparing for a long time, but he was still unsure, so he came over and asked:

> "Can you pass this interview?"

The traditional six-yao method is as follows:

1. Take out three copper coins, calm down and explain the problem clearly in your mind;
2. Shake it six times and write down the front and back of the coin each time;
3. Draw a hexagram based on the results of six times;
4. According to fixed rules, "install" the five elements, six relatives, six gods, and world responses on this hexagram;
5. Find the line (Yong Shen) that represents "work" and see if it is prosperous and whether it is being supported or harmed;
6. Give a judgment: whether the hope is high, when there will be news, and what to pay attention to.

This process sounds mysterious, but it can actually be broken down into three very simple things:

- **Get a hexagram in a random way** (start hexagram);
- **"Decorate" the hexagrams into an information table according to fixed rules** (decorate the hexagrams);
- **Read this table according to the Five Elements' hexagram (Duan Gua).

In this chapter, we will make these three things clear.

## 1.1 One sentence definition

> **Liu Yao is a traditional divination method that uses the sixty-four hexagrams of the Book of Changes as the symbol system and the five elements as the rule to infer the development trend of something by starting, installing, and breaking hexagrams. **

Taking it apart, there are three key words:

| Keywords | Meaning | Analogy |
| ------ | ----------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Symbols | Sixty-four hexagrams, each with six layers of yin and yang | An alphabet of only 64 words |
| Rules | The five elements are in conflict, prosperity and decline, and conflict and combination | A set of grammar |
| Operation | Starting a hexagram → Installing a hexagram → Breaking a hexagram | The process of writing an article |

Liuyao does not require you to believe that "copper coins have spirits". It is more like a **symbolic operation system**: the input is a randomly obtained hexagram, the middle is a bunch of determined rules, and the output is an interpretable judgment.

## 1.2 Three-layer structure of Liuyao| Levels | What to do | Random or deterministic | Difficulty |
| ---------- | ------------------------------- | ---------- | ---- |
| **Start the hexagram layer** | Shake the copper coin six times to get the hexagram and moving lines | Random | Low |
| **Gua Layer** | Najia, Ding Liuqin, An Liushen, Paishiying | Completely determined | Medium |
| **Duan Gua Layer** | Use gods, see prosperity and decline, discuss birth and restraint, and make judgments | Rules + Experience | High |

This structure is important because it explains why Liuyao is "both objective and subjective":

- **The pretend hexagram layer is objective**: For the same hexagram and the same day, the Najia, Liuqin and Shiying pretended by anyone should be the same;
- **The Duan Gua layer is subjective**: what to look at first, what to look at later, which signal is more important, different people have different weight distributions.

Therefore, the first step in learning Liu Yao is not to learn "how to break", but to learn "how to pretend" - only by doing the certain parts correctly can you be qualified to talk about the uncertain parts.

## 1.3 The origin of Liu Yao (minimalist version)

| Period | What happened | What it means today |
| -------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Pre-Qin Dynasty | "Book of Changes" uses yarrow to form hexagrams, with hexagram words and line words | Provides a symbol system of sixty-four hexagrams |
| Western Han Dynasty | Jingfang Chuangnajia, Baigong, Liuqin, Shiying | The birth of the "metadata layer" of Liuyao |
| Between the Tang and Song Dynasties | "Fire Pearl Forest" promoted "replacing money with water chestnuts" | Shaking three copper coins six times became the mainstream hexagram method |
| Ming and Qing Dynasties | "Additional and Delete Divination Book of Changes" and "Divination Zhengzong" are mature | The hexagram system of using gods, the moon to build the sun, the prosperity and decline, and the corresponding period is finalized |

Here we would like to clarify a common misunderstanding:

> **Six Yao does not mean "using the hexagrams and lines of the Book of Changes to tell fortunes". **The six lines borrow the hexagram symbols from the Book of Changes, but the Duan hexagram mainly looks at the five elements, six relatives, prosperity and decline, and birth and restraint, rather than interpreting the hexagrams. In other words, it is a set of "Najia Divination Method" developed on the symbol system of "Book of Changes", and it is no longer the same set of operations as the pre-Qin yarrow divination method.

## 1.4 Six Yao is not easy to count with plum blossoms, nor is it eight characters.

Beginners are most likely to mix these things up. A table makes it clear:

| | Six Yao | Easy Counting of Plum Blossoms | Eight Characters |
| ------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------- |
| How to start the hexagram/start the disk | Shake three copper coins six times | Time, numbers, external responses | Date, month, day and hour of birth |
| Core tools | Six relatives, five elements, prosperity and decline, world response | Hexagrams, body functions, external responses | Heavenly stems and earthly branches, ten gods, great luck |
| Good at | Short-term trends of specific matters | Quick image acquisition and flexibility | Life-long pattern and long-term trends |
| Learning characteristics | Many rules, but systematic | Quick to get started, difficult to master | Huge system, long cycle |

**This handout only talks about the Six Yao (Najia Zhenfa)** and does not involve the easy counting of plum blossoms and the eight characters.

## 1.5 What can and cannot be done by Liu Yao

### What can be done

- **Structuring vague questions**: Forcing you to change "I'm so confused recently" into "Is it appropriate for me to change jobs this month?";
- **Multiple angles provided**: Yongshen, Shiying, Yuesiya, and Changbian, each representing a different perspective;
- **Assisted Review**: Record the hexagrams and subsequent facts to train your judgment;
- **Cultural Learning**: Understand how ancient people used symbol systems to deal with uncertainty.

### What can’t be done

- **Accurately predict the future**: It gives a sense of trends and probability, not a definite answer;
- **Alternative to professional advice**: For medical, legal, investment, and psychological issues, please seek professional advice;
- **Accuracy Guaranteed**: If different people interpret the same hexagram, the conclusions may be completely different;
- **Make decisions for others**: The hexagrams are a reference, and the right to choose is always in people's hands.> **Egg Tart Reminder**: If a person tells you "This hexagram 100% means that you will definitely...", no matter how famous he is, this sentence itself is not in line with the tradition of Liu Yao - Liu Yao talks about prosperity and decline, trends, and due dates, not a straightforward statement.
